Abstract: A method performed by a radio base station to prevent at least one radio unit, associated with the radio base station, from overheating The method comprises predicting (401) overheating instances considering environmental impact and network attributes, proactively identifying (402) overheating actuations based on the predicted overheating instances, and proactively taking (403) temperature remedy actions when an overheating actuation has been identified and before the predicted overheating has occurred to prevent the radio unit from overheating.
